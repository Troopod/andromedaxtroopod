3Approach

Every area will be assessed on four tests.

ScopeWhat the spend buys
UsageHow much of it is used
MarketComparison with at least three vendor quotes or benchmarks
ContractTerms, lock-in and SLAs

Each finding will be reported in the following form:

  1. Observation
  2. Impact (₹)
  3. Root cause
  4. Recommendation
  5. Management response
  6. Owner and target date

7Assumptions and points to confirm

The plan rests on the assumptions below; please confirm or correct them at kickoff.

  • Cost figures shared are monthly (for example, CISO ₹3.5 L per month = ₹42 L per year).
  • Working days are Monday to Friday; 25 December and 1 January are holidays.
  • Andromeda will nominate a single point of contact (CTO / IT head) to coordinate data and meetings.
  • The on-site visit on 15–18 December will be at Andromeda’s office, with area owners available as scheduled.
  • Data requested above is shared by 11 December; a delay will move later milestones by the same period.
